## 🛠️ Everyday Workflow

A simple everyday workflow looks like this:

1. Open your campaign workspace and review active campaigns.
2. Add new campaign ideas as they come up.
3. Group related ad sets and creative notes under the right campaign.
4. Update budget notes when planned spend changes.
5. Record performance observations after checking campaign results.
6. Prepare reporting notes for weekly or monthly reviews.
7. Archive finished campaign details so the workspace stays tidy.

This flow helps you avoid scattered notes and makes it easier to return to a campaign after a break. You can also use the same structure for different advertising platforms, which keeps your process familiar even when the campaign type changes.

## 📊 Reporting and Performance Notes

Reporting is often the part of advertising work that gets delayed. fb-andgads-manager gives reporting notes a clear place inside the campaign workspace. You can record what changed, what you observed, and what you plan to do next. That makes it easier to prepare a summary without rebuilding the story from memory.

Performance notes can include campaign results, creative observations, audience notes, and budget changes. The goal is not to replace a full reporting tool. The goal is to keep the important context close to the campaign so your reporting workflow starts from an organized place.
